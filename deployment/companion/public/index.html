02 / REPRODUCIBLE METHOD

From source data to decisions you can inspect.

Each stage preserves provenance,
assumptions, and evidence limits.

  1. 01

    Prepare & validate

    Preserve the 18 order fields, join five factory coordinates and 15 product assignments, audit dates and outliers, and retain unmapped demand.

  2. 02

    Explore & diagnose

    Analyze demand, sales, source gross profit and geographic distance. Compare Linear Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting using chronological splits; anomalous dates make these models diagnostic only.

  3. 03

    Simulate & rank

    Replay mapped orders across alternate factories with documented hypothetical logistics costs. Rank speed and contribution tradeoffs, enforce supplied compatibility and capacity limits, and combine common sensitivity shocks.

  4. 04

    Investigate before execution

    Use ranked alternatives to prioritize evidence collection. Verify actual origin, dispatch performance, capacity and production capability before considering operational reassignment.
